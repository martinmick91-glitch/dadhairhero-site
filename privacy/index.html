Privacy Policy — Dad Hair Hero Last updated: [09/09/2026] Dad Hair Hero does not collect, transmit or store your personal data on any server. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. What the app stores. Everything you enter — your child's name, hair type and age band, your progress, and any photos you add to the style diary — is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. Photos. The app asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to add a photo to the diary. Photos are saved in the app's own storage on your device. If you share a photo using your phone's share options, it goes wherever you send it — never to us. Links to shops. The Kit section contains affiliate links to product pages. Tapping one opens your normal browser, and we may earn a small commission on purchases. Once you leave the app, the site you land on has its own privacy policy, which we don't control. Deleting your data. Settings contains a "Delete everything" option that removes all stored data and diary photos from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes everything. Children. This app is designed for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Contact. hello@dadhairhero.com
